Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Mise is a personal recipe app. It has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't send your data anywhere except the Google services described below.

Without signing in

Everything Mise remembers stays in your browser on that device: your favorites, recently opened recipes, checked-off ingredients, running timers and theme. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

The recipes themselves are read from a public file in Google Drive. Recipe photos are loaded from Google Drive too.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It keeps your favorites and recently opened recipes the same on every device. Mise asks Google for two things:

  • Your email address, shown in the app so you know which account you're using.
  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive, a hidden space only Mise can see. Mise saves one small file there with your favorites and recent recipes. It can't see or change any of your other Drive files.

Your sign-in stays in your browser. Favorites and recents are stored only in your browser and your Google Drive.

Adding recipes

Only the owner of this recipe book can add recipes. When they do, the link, recipe text or photos they add are sent to Mise's small server, which fetches the page and sends the recipe to Anthropic's Claude API to convert it. The finished recipe is saved in the recipe file in Google Drive. Nothing else about you is sent.

Removing your data

  • Sign out from the account button in Mise. Your favorites stay on that device.
  • To remove Mise's access, go to your Google Account's third-party connections and remove it.
  • To delete the saved file, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, and use Delete hidden app data for Mise.

Questions

Mise's code is public. For questions, open an issue on its GitHub page.
