Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Mise is a personal recipe app. It has no accounts of its own and no ads. It doesn't send your data anywhere except the services described below: Google, for recipes and sign-in, and PostHog, for visit statistics.

Without signing in

Everything Mise remembers stays in your browser on that device: your favorites, recently opened recipes, checked-off ingredients, running timers and theme. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

The recipes themselves are read from a public file in Google Drive. Recipe photos are loaded from Google Drive too.

Visit statistics

If you aren't signed in, Mise uses PostHog to learn how the site is used, so it can be made better. It records:

  • Which screens and recipes you open, what you search for, which filters you use and which timers you start.
  • Where you click and scroll, shown as heatmaps.
  • Recordings of visits, replaying what was on screen. What you type into boxes is hidden from the recordings.
  • Your rough location from your IP address, and your browser and device type.

PostHog keeps a random ID in your browser to tell visits apart. It isn't linked to your name or email, and it isn't used for advertising or sold. Signed-in visitors aren't tracked at all. An ad or tracker blocker stops all of this; Mise works the same either way.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It keeps your favorites, recently opened recipes and shopping list the same on every device. Mise asks Google for two things:

  • Your first name and email address, shown in the app so you know which account you're using.
  • Its own app-data folder in your Google Drive, a hidden space only Mise can see. Mise saves one small file there with your favorites, recent recipes and shopping list. It can't see or change any of your other Drive files.

Your sign-in stays in your browser. Favorites, recents and your shopping list are stored only in your browser and your Google Drive.

Adding recipes

Only the owner of this recipe book can add recipes. When they do, the link, recipe text or photos they add are sent to Mise's small server, which fetches the page and sends the recipe to Anthropic's Claude API to convert it. The finished recipe is saved in the recipe file in Google Drive. Nothing else about you is sent.

Removing your data

  • Sign out from the account button in Mise. Your favorites stay on that device.
  • To remove Mise's access, go to your Google Account's third-party connections and remove it.
  • To delete the saved file, open Google Drive settings, choose Manage apps, and use Delete hidden app data for Mise.

Questions

Mise's code is public. For questions, open an issue on its GitHub page.
